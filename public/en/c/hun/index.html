If you were born in Hungary

Right now, about 0.06% of the babies born on Earth are born in Hungary: 1 in 1,545, about 85,373 a year, or one every 370 minutes.

The chance that a baby born in Hungary dies before turning five is 0.4%, about 1.7 times lower than the United States (0.7%). Life expectancy is 80.2 years for women and 73.7 years for men.
