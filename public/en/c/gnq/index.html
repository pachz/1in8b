If you were born in Equatorial Guinea

Right now, about 0.04% of the babies born on Earth are born in Equatorial Guinea: 1 in 2,412, about 54,694 a year, or one every 577 minutes.

The chance that a baby born in Equatorial Guinea dies before turning five is 7.0%, about 11 times that of the United States (0.7%). Life expectancy is 65.7 years for women and 61.7 years for men.
