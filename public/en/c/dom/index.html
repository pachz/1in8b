If you were born in the Dominican Republic

Right now, about 0.15% of the babies born on Earth are born in the Dominican Republic: 1 in 649, about 203,183 a year, or one every 155 minutes.

The chance that a baby born in the Dominican Republic dies before turning five is 3.1%, about 4.8 times that of the United States (0.7%). Life expectancy is 76.9 years for women and 70.4 years for men.
