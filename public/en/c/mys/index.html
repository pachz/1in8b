If you were born in Malaysia

Right now, about 0.33% of the babies born on Earth are born in Malaysia: 1 in 303, about 435,989 a year, or one every 72 minutes.

The chance that a baby born in Malaysia dies before turning five is 0.8%, about 1.3 times that of the United States (0.7%). Life expectancy is 79.1 years for women and 74.2 years for men.
