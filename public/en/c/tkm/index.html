If you were born in Turkmenistan

Right now, about 0.12% of the babies born on Earth are born in Turkmenistan: 1 in 824, about 160,100 a year, or one every 197 minutes.

The chance that a baby born in Turkmenistan dies before turning five is 4.0%, about 6.2 times that of the United States (0.7%). Life expectancy is 73.8 years for women and 66.3 years for men.
