If you were born in Ukraine

Right now, about 0.16% of the babies born on Earth are born in Ukraine: 1 in 622, about 212,231 a year, or one every 149 minutes.

The chance that a baby born in Ukraine dies before turning five is 0.8%, about the same as the United States (0.7%). Life expectancy is 78.3 years for women and 68.3 years for men.
