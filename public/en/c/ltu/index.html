If you were born in Lithuania

Right now, about 0.02% of the babies born on Earth are born in Lithuania: 1 in 6,155, about 21,434 a year, or one every 1,472 minutes.

The chance that a baby born in Lithuania dies before turning five is 0.3%, about 1.9 times lower than the United States (0.7%). Life expectancy is 80.7 years for women and 71.2 years for men.
