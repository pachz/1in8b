If you were born in Paraguay

Right now, about 0.10% of the babies born on Earth are born in Paraguay: 1 in 965, about 136,778 a year, or one every 231 minutes.

The chance that a baby born in Paraguay dies before turning five is 1.7%, about 2.6 times that of the United States (0.7%). Life expectancy is 77.0 years for women and 70.5 years for men.
