If you were born in Benin

Right now, about 0.36% of the babies born on Earth are born in Benin: 1 in 276, about 478,062 a year, or one every 66 minutes.

The chance that a baby born in Benin dies before turning five is 7.7%, about 12 times that of the United States (0.7%). Life expectancy is 62.8 years for women and 58.8 years for men.
