If you were born in Saudi Arabia

Right now, about 0.41% of the babies born on Earth are born in Saudi Arabia: 1 in 242, about 546,038 a year, or about 1 a minute.

The chance that a baby born in Saudi Arabia dies before turning five is 0.6%, about the same as the United States (0.7%). Life expectancy is 80.2 years for women and 77.2 years for men.
