If you were born in the United States

Right now, about 2.77% of the babies born on Earth are born in the United States: 1 in 36, about 3.7 million a year, or about 7 a minute.

The chance that a baby born in the United States dies before turning five is 0.7%. Life expectancy is 81.9 years for women and 76.5 years for men.
