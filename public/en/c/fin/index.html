If you were born in Finland

Right now, about 0.03% of the babies born on Earth are born in Finland: 1 in 3,029, about 43,556 a year, or one every 725 minutes.

The chance that a baby born in Finland dies before turning five is 0.2%, about 2.7 times lower than the United States (0.7%). Life expectancy is 84.3 years for women and 79.5 years for men.
