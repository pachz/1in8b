If you were born in Slovenia

Right now, about 0.01% of the babies born on Earth are born in Slovenia: 1 in 7,370, about 17,902 a year, or one every 1,763 minutes.

The chance that a baby born in Slovenia dies before turning five is 0.2%, about 2.8 times lower than the United States (0.7%). Life expectancy is 84.2 years for women and 78.8 years for men.
