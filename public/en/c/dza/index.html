If you were born in Algeria

Right now, about 0.69% of the babies born on Earth are born in Algeria: 1 in 146, about 905,981 a year, or about 1.7 a minute.

The chance that a baby born in Algeria dies before turning five is 2.2%, about 3.4 times that of the United States (0.7%). Life expectancy is 77.3 years for women and 75.3 years for men.
