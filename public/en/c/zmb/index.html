If you were born in Zambia

Right now, about 0.52% of the babies born on Earth are born in Zambia: 1 in 192, about 685,565 a year, or about 1.3 a minute.

The chance that a baby born in Zambia dies before turning five is 4.9%, about 7.6 times that of the United States (0.7%). Life expectancy is 68.3 years for women and 64.3 years for men.
