If you were born in Uruguay

Right now, about 0.03% of the babies born on Earth are born in Uruguay: 1 in 3,945, about 33,443 a year, or one every 944 minutes.

The chance that a baby born in Uruguay dies before turning five is 0.7%, about the same as the United States (0.7%). Life expectancy is 81.3 years for women and 74.8 years for men.
