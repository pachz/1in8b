If you were born in Pakistan

Right now, about 5.22% of the babies born on Earth are born in Pakistan: 1 in 19, about 6.9 million a year, or about 13.1 a minute.

The chance that a baby born in Pakistan dies before turning five is 5.8%, about 8.9 times that of the United States (0.7%). Life expectancy is 69.8 years for women and 65.3 years for men.
