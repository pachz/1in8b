If you were born in the United Arab Emirates

Right now, about 0.08% of the babies born on Earth are born in the United Arab Emirates: 1 in 1,268, about 104,015 a year, or one every 303 minutes.

The chance that a baby born in the United Arab Emirates dies before turning five is 0.5%, about 1.3 times lower than the United States (0.7%). Life expectancy is 84.1 years for women and 81.6 years for men.
