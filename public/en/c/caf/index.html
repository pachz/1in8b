If you were born in the Central African Republic

Right now, about 0.18% of the babies born on Earth are born in the Central African Republic: 1 in 552, about 238,858 a year, or one every 132 minutes.

The chance that a baby born in the Central African Republic dies before turning five is 9.3%, about 14 times that of the United States (0.7%). Life expectancy is 59.4 years for women and 55.4 years for men.
