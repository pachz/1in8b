2. The life simulation

  • 1 در ۸ میلیارد is free from start to finish: no accounts, no ads, nothing sold inside it, and paying never changes a life in the simulation.
  • The stories are fiction built on real statistics. They are not medical, legal or financial advice.
  • It is made for ages 15 and up. Following real statistics, death, illness, war and poverty happen, and so do drinking, smoking, gambling, prison, violence at home and early marriage; a past world also tells of real history such as wars and mass killings. Drug dependence in adult lives, and sexual violence from age 15, also happen as often as the real statistics say, never shown in detail or how, always with where to find help, and not on shared cards or memorials. Suicide also happens from the teenage years on, as often as the real statistics say, without showing how or why, and shared cards and memorials do not name it. Where the law allows it, a family member's death with a doctor's help also happens at the real rate, never as a choice and without saying whether it is right or wrong, and not on shared cards or memorials.

Streaming and videos as above, and the works you make with keepsakes, stay free to use without asking, as now. Ideas such as being born by real statistics and living one life belong to no one: you are free to make a new work of your own from them.
  • Life records are kept only on your device (browser storage). They can disappear if you clear the site data, if your browser runs out of space, or in browsers that clear data of sites not visited for a while on their own, such as Safari. 4. Refunds

  • After 30 days we still refund when: it does not work for a technical reason we cannot fix, it is not as described, you paid twice for the same thing by mistake, or a minor bought it without a parent or guardian's permission.
  • To ask for a refund, write to customerservice@1in8b.ir with just the email you paid with. You do not need to give a reason. We usually handle it within 3 business days. You can also ask Link support from your receipt email.
  • If the law where you paid, or Link's refund policy, gives you wider rights, those rights apply.
  • After a refund (or a payment reversed through a card dispute) the pass can no longer make anything new. Films, album and record book files already saved, and memorials already up, stay as they are; a memorial can be taken down from the device that made it at any time.

5. If the service changes or ends

If what the pass offers is reduced or the service has to end, we announce it on the first screen and in the update notes at least 30 days before. If the service ends, passes bought in the 90 days before are refunded on request. Films and albums you saved stay on your device.

6. Minors

1 در ۸ میلیارد is made for ages 15 and up. Players under 15 should use it with a parent or guardian.

Children under 14 should not use the features that keep personal information (shared lives, feedback, the keepsake pass). The keepsake pass must be paid for by an adult; minors should ask a parent or guardian to pay. If a minor paid without permission, tell us and we will refund it.

Teachers who want to use it in class can open classroom mode from 'باز کردن در حالت کلاس' at the bottom of the first screen. Classroom mode holds back content by our own guideline of ages 12 and up and turns off every feature that keeps personal information, and payments. In classroom mode, the teacher should watch and guide alongside the pupils.
