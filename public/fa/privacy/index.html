1. What we handle, why, how and for how long

Life records (the life you are living, the list of past lives and the full record of the latest 10 (of every past life in a browser with a keepsake pass, or in one that received the records moved from such a browser), your notes, settings, recent problems on this device (where a script error happened, a save, opening, payment check, memorial, video or move that did not work with a short code for why, the version and the time; 30 days, up to 12), the time crisis words were seen (for 30 days after it, no life on this device ends by or meets a suicide))

Purpose
Continuing a life, reopening past lives, reporting a problem
How it is kept
On your device (browser storage). If you have a keepsake pass and turn on "Keep my past lives on the server too", a copy of that browser's records is also kept on our server (see "Keeping past lives on the server" below). Recent problems are never sent by themselves; they go along only when you send feedback or write to us with "Report a problem". Part of a life reaches our server only when you choose: when you share it (shared lives) or make a share link (the country, age and sex in the simulation, or, when you share “The life I wish for” or a life’s grain, its name, are in the link), or move your lives to another browser or phone (see "Moving my lives" below), or put up a memorial or carry a life with your keepsake pass (see the keepsake rows below)
How long
Until you clear the site data in your browser (some browsers, such as Safari, clear data of sites not visited for a while on their own)

Anonymous usage statistics: kinds of screens and actions (a life begins or ends, etc.) with details from the simulation (birth country, the country lived in now after a move abroad, age, auto mode, speed, the world chosen: past, today or future, and for a past world the decade of birth; when a life ends, the "today's theme" of the day it began and whether the life met it), how you arrived (link source, in-app browser type), device type and screen width, country of access (a country code derived from the connection; the address itself is not kept), a random number made fresh for each tab

Purpose
Understanding use, finding errors, managing server costs, the "living now" map on the first screen
How it is kept
Stored as hourly totals. The random number and the tab's current state are used only for the "online now" view and change on reload. A visible tab sends its signal every 9 minutes, to cut server requests, and a page left untouched for over 20 minutes sends none. No cookies, advertising IDs or IP addresses are stored
How long
Totals that cannot identify anyone; the "online now" view (random number included) forgets a tab within 10 minutes of its last signal

Script error reports: version, screen, where the error happened and a short fingerprint (no error text, no address)

Purpose
Fixing errors
How it is kept
Hourly totals
How long
As above

Moving my lives (optional): the life records in this browser (the list of past lives, the full record of the latest 10 (of every past life in a browser with a keepsake pass), the life in progress, your notes and farewells), the numbers and take-down keys of memorials put up from this device, the "country you were really born in" you chose, and whether this browser has a keepsake pass (the pass key is not sent)

Purpose
Moving your lives to another app's browser or a new phone (instead of a login)
How it is kept
Kept briefly on our server under a random code that works once. Nothing that says whose records they are (an account, an email, an IP address) is stored with them. Anyone holding the code can take them, so do not send it to anyone else
How long
Deleted as soon as they are taken, or after 3 days

Keeping past lives on the server (keepsakes, optional): the life records of a browser where it is turned on (what "Moving my lives" carries: the list of past lives, the full record of the lives you can see again, the life in progress, your notes and farewells, the numbers and take-down keys of memorials put up from that device, and the "country you were really born in" you chose)

Purpose
Bringing them back by opening the pass again after the browser's data is cleared, or in another browser
How it is kept
If you have a keepsake pass and turn on "Keep my past lives on the server too", that browser's records are compressed and kept on our server (Cloudflare). Only the latest copy of each browser is kept (up to three browsers a pass), under the pass's hash and a random number each browser makes, and it is replaced when a life ends and about every 10 minutes during one. No email or IP address is stored with it. Nothing is sent unless you turn it on
How long
Kept while you have the pass; deleted at once when you turn it off or the pass is refunded (from the copies kept against failures within 30 days, see section 5)

1in8b Engine API (for developers, optional): the number of calls by kind of answer. With a key (given from November 1, 2026): the key's hash, its plan, when it was made, the month's use, and the hash of the email address it was sent to (one key an address)

Purpose
Giving and checking API keys, preventing abuse and keeping server costs in check, checking a key's monthly limit
How it is kept
Calls are stored only as hourly totals, without who made them. A key is sent by our server as a reply when you write to api@1in8b.ir, and that email is also forwarded to the operator's Gmail, as in the Email row below. Our server (Cloudflare) keeps the key and the email address only as irreversible hashes, never the key itself, the address or a name. How many calls may be made in a minute is counted by Cloudflare for that minute only; the operator stores nothing of it
How long
Totals are kept in a form that identifies no one · a key's record is deleted at once when the key is turned off (by writing again with "stop" in the subject, or on request; from the copies kept against failures within 30 days, see section 5)

Email (optional): your email address and message

Purpose
Replying, handling payments, refunds and problems
How it is kept
Mail to 1in8b.ir addresses is forwarded by Cloudflare Email Routing to the operator's Gmail (Google) and kept there. If you write through "Report a problem", the version, device type and screen width, the state of your current life, recent problems on this device and (if you have a pass) its check code are filled into the message, and you can read and delete them before sending. From October 18, 2026, the operator reads and sorts these emails with the AI assistant Claude (Anthropic), checks the related payment records and drafts replies; the operator checks every reply before sending it and never leaves a decision such as a refund to the AI (see section 3). As it forwards mail to customerservice@, the server reads the subject and the start of the message and turns the sender's address and up to three addresses written in the message, with the forms they are likely to have been mistyped as at checkout (such as .con for .com), into irreversible hashes to find a keepsake payment. If the email asks about a refund, comes from an address that bought a keepsake pass (unless it was refunded), or seems to come from someone who paid with a mistyped form of their address, it sends the operator's Gmail one more email through Cloudflare's email sending, with the sender's name and address, the subject, the start of the message (up to 1,500 characters), the payment found (date and amount), that pass's purchases, refunds and memorials, what got in the way of its memorials in the last 30 days (see Keepsake pass below) and a draft reply. If an email from the very address that paid clearly asks for a refund within 30 days of payment, the server asks Stripe to refund that payment (sending only the payment number) and replies to the sender. The server never decides against a refund; the operator does. The server does not store the addresses or the message
How long
Deleted within a year after it is handled; payment and refund questions may be kept up to 3 years in case of disputes; quotes and summaries in the development records of what was fixed follow the GitHub row in section 3

4. Children and teenagers

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14 (16 in some countries). Please do not write names, contact details, schools or where you live in shared lives or feedback; we try to filter such details out automatically, though no filter catches everything, and if we learn that a child's personal information has reached us, we delete it. Under the terms of Link, the seller, the keepsake pass must be paid for by an adult; minors should ask a parent or guardian to pay. If a minor paid without a parent or guardian's permission, write to customerservice@1in8b.ir for a refund.

For parents and guardians. 1 در ۸ میلیارد is a life simulation made for ages 15 and up. It lives one person's life by real statistics, so death, illness, war and poverty, drinking, smoking, gambling, prison, violence at home and early marriage happen as they do in reality, and a past world tells of real history such as wars and mass killings. Drug dependence in adult lives, and sexual violence from age 15, also happen as often as the real statistics say, never shown in detail or how, always with where to find help, and never on shared cards or memorials (suicide happens from the teenage years on, as often as the real statistics say, never showing how or why, never named on shared cards or memorials, and always with where to find help). Players cannot talk to or contact each other, there are no ads, and paying never changes a life in the simulation. For players under 15, consider trying it together; questions are welcome at contact@1in8b.ir.

Classroom mode. Teachers who want to use it in class can open classroom mode from 'باز کردن در حالت کلاس' at the bottom of the first screen. A browser opened that way holds back content by our own guideline of ages 12 and up (no suicide, no violence or physical punishment at home, no drug dependence or sexual violence, no assisted dying in the family, no choosing to drink, smoke, gamble or commit a crime, and only today's world), and turns off shared lives, feedback, problem reports, moving records, keepsakes and memorials. So in classroom mode no names, contact details or pupils' words reach our server, and what is written in the pauses and parting words stays on the device. Only anonymous statistics that identify no one are sent, and not even those if the browser has "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control turned on. That classroom mode is on is kept in that browser only.

5. Deletion

Information is deleted without delay once its period ends or its purpose is met, in a way that cannot be recovered.

Copies against failures. So that nothing is lost to a failure or a mistake, the server's records (keepsake payments and passes, memorials, shared lives, anonymous statistics and operating records) have copies. Cloudflare, which runs the server, keeps 30 days of changes so the server can go back to a point within them; once a day we copy the records to storage inside the same Cloudflare account and keep each copy for 14 days; and we may download a copy from the admin page and keep it encrypted (deleted after 30 days). So a post or memorial you delete or take down is gone from the site and the server at once, and from the copies within 30 days. Copies are used only to bring back records lost to a failure or a mistake.

6. Your rights

  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop or object to its processing: write to privacy@1in8b.ir. We reply within 10 days.
  • You can delete a shared life yourself from the device you posted it on. A memorial can be taken down from the device that made it. What you delete is gone at once (from the copies kept against failures within 30 days, see section 5).
  • Life records on your device disappear when you clear the site data in your browser.
  • Anonymous statistics are totals that cannot identify anyone, so one person's part cannot be singled out.
  • You may also complain to your data protection authority.
  • If you are in Brazil, you have the rights of Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD), exercised the same way: confirmation that we process your data, access, correction, anonymization or deletion, portability, information on whom it is shared with, and withdrawing consent. You may also complain to Brazil's data protection authority (ANPD).

7. Cookies and similar technology

We set no cookies (Cloudflare, which delivers the site, may set a strictly necessary security cookie, and the checkout uses cookies under Stripe's policy). Life records and settings are kept in browser storage (localStorage, and IndexedDB for the full records of past lives with a keepsake pass) and leave your device only when you share or carry a life, as set out in section 1. With "Keep my past lives on the server too" turned on in the keepsakes, a copy of that browser's records is also kept on our server (section 1). You can block or clear site data in your browser settings; continuing a life and past lives then will not work.

8. Security

All connections are encrypted (HTTPS), we handle only what is needed, IP addresses are not stored, the admin page needs a secret key, and a content security policy blocks outside scripts. Our server handles email addresses only as hashes.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we announce it on this page and in the update notes that day, and changes that work against you, such as handling more information or sharing it with more providers, at least 7 days before.

Coming change (from October 31, 2026): the anonymous usage statistics add whether a visit is the first time this browser opens the site. That is known only from a mark left in this browser (one8b.seen.v1, with nothing else in it), and the server keeps only whether the visit was a first one, in hourly totals like the other statistics. It is to tell new visitors from returning ones in understanding how the site is used.
